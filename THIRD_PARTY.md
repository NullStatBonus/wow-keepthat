# Third-party notices and data sources

KeepThat's code is MIT licensed (see `LICENSE`). The sources below are credited for the facts in
`KeepThat/Data/`. No text, images or code from them is copied; only game facts (item and recipe IDs,
skill ranges, craft counts) are recorded, and every material count is computed from the client's
own recipe data.

## Ace3 libraries

`KeepThat/Libs/` holds an unmodified subset of Ace3 (https://github.com/WoWUIDev/Ace3). See
`KeepThat/Libs/LICENSE-Ace3.txt` and `KeepThat/Libs/VERSIONS.txt`.

## Game data (recipes, reagents, item and spell names)

Generated by `tools/build-catalog.py` from the client's DB2 tables (SkillLineAbility, SpellReagents,
SpellEffect, SpellName, ItemSparse) as published by wago.tools (https://wago.tools):

| Client | Build |
|---|---|
| Classic Era | 1.15.9.70576 |
| WoW Forever | 1.60.1.71139 |

World of Warcraft game data is the property of Blizzard Entertainment.

## Endgame material lists (`tools/endgame/`)

Curated for KeepThat and reviewed by the maintainer (2026-11-15), each item linked to its Wowhead
page (https://www.wowhead.com/classic). The WoW Forever lists are a copy of the Classic Era lists until
Forever's new content and guides settle.

## DFL Profession Journal

When DFL Profession Journal is installed, KeepThat reads its route data in game. Nothing from it is
copied into KeepThat's files or saved variables (its license is All Rights Reserved).
